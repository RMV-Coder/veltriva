## Install and remove

- Per user, no administrator rights: it installs into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs`, adds Start-menu and desktop shortcuts, and starts Veltriva.
- Your tabs, history and settings live in `%APPDATA%\Veltriva`. Uninstalling asks whether to remove them too; installing a newer version keeps them.

## What's new

The first release of Veltriva Browser, a local-first Windows browser for people who keep 50 or 100 tabs open. What it does is in the [README](../../README.md); the highlights:

![The Veltriva window: open tabs, a tab just moved to Sleeping with its Undo toast, and the Archive, beside a web page](../images/sleeping-archive.png)

![The memory palette: three letters find pages that are open, sleeping and archived](../images/memory-palette.png)

- **Tabs that look after themselves.**
  - A tab hidden for an hour is unloaded to free its memory, and comes back when you select it: its history, where you had scrolled, and, in the same run, what you typed.
  - Tabs you stopped using move to **Sleeping**, then the **Archive**. The time counts your use of the computer, so a weekend away changes nothing.
  - Anything that might hold your work is left alone: sound, the camera, unsaved changes, a sign-in window.
  - For your first 3 days, **Preview** shows what automation would move before it moves anything. Every move has **Undo**, and **Activity** says why it happened.
- **A memory you can search.**
  - Every page you visit is remembered in your profile only.
  - **Ctrl+Shift+K** finds a page by a few partial words, whether it's open, Sleeping, archived or long closed, and brings it back.
  - The address bar suggests pages from it as you type.
- **Your session survives.** Tabs, their history and the window come back after a restart or a crash. After two crashes in a row, safe mode opens your tabs without loading them.
- **Privacy and control.**
  - No telemetry, no account, no cloud, no remote suggestions.
  - Settings has site rules, **Pause memory**, **Clear data** (rewritten, not just hidden), **Forget a site**, site permissions and how long pages are kept.
  - The update check is **off by default**, and makes no request until you turn it on.
- **A complete everyday browser:** find, zoom, print, downloads with safe names, permission prompts that name the site, pop-ups only after a click, PDFs, DevTools, fullscreen and context menus.
- **Keyboard and screen readers.** Everything works from the keyboard: F6 cycles the address bar, the sidebar and the page. Changes are announced to screen readers, and the interface is checked against WCAG 2.2 AA in the light and dark themes.
- **Diagnostics that stay on your machine:** `veltriva://diagnostics` (memory per tab, what automation did, the log) and an export for problem reports that holds no page titles. `veltriva://about` shows the versions and licenses.
- **Measured:**
  - Search answers in 10.3 ms at the 95th percentile with 50,000 pages remembered ([docs/benchmarks/search.md](../benchmarks/search.md), on a laptop under load).
  - Unloading frees about 34 MB per tab: with 100 tabs, 3,771 MB all live against 383 MB with 99 unloaded ([docs/benchmarks/memory.md](../benchmarks/memory.md), median of 5 runs, on the same laptop under load).
